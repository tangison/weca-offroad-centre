# PRODUCT — Weca Offroad Centre

## What the business is

Weca Offroad Centre is a Namibian 4x4 accessories and fitment business based in
Swakopmund Industrial. Founded in 2015 by Werner Schaap, the business sells and
fits rooftop tents, suspension, bumpers, canopies, lighting, camping gear,
tyres, recovery equipment, winches, awnings, refrigeration, power systems and
navigation hardware for overlanding and work-ready 4x4 vehicles.

The business is a retail shop plus a fitment workshop. Customers browse
products, request a quote, and book fitment. Pricing is in Namibian dollars
(N$).

## Who it serves

- Namibian overlanders and weekend campers
- Mining and contractor fleet operators needing heavy-duty suspension and
  protection on Land Cruiser 70 series and Hilux
- Tour operators building safari vehicles
- Farmers needing rugged work 4x4s
- International overlanders passing through Swakopmund

## What the website does

1. **Shop** — 236 products across 14 categories, with search, category filter,
   sort, and a quote-by-WhatsApp enquiry flow. No online checkout: the business
   model is quote-then-fit, so the conversion action is "request quote".
2. **Services** — fitment services with starting prices and durations.
3. **Gallery** — finished builds showing workshop capability.
4. **Testimonials** — verified customer reviews.
5. **About** — owner story, location, hours, business credentials.
6. **Contact** — address, phones, WhatsApp, email, embedded map, contact form.

## What the website does NOT do

- Online payment or shopping cart. The business closes sales by quote and
  invoice, not by card.
- User accounts or login.
- Inventory-managed stock levels. `inStock` is a boolean display flag.
- Product search beyond name, brand and description substring.
