# CONTENT_PLAN - Weca Offroad Centre

## Source of truth

All factual content comes from `BUSINESS_INFO.md` (owner, address, phones,
hours, social links) and from the existing approved copy already shipped in
`src/lib/data.ts` and the route pages. No content was invented beyond the
catalog expansion documented in `BUILD_PLAN.md`.

## Catalog content rules

- Product names follow the pattern `<Brand> <Model> <Variant>` (e.g.
  "Tentco Classic Rooftop Tent", "Warn Zeon 10-S 10000lb").
- Prices are in N$ with thousand separators, no decimals (e.g. `N$ 28,500`).
- Descriptions are one sentence stating what the product is and who makes it.
- Features are 3–5 short factual bullets, no marketing fluff.
- Brand names are real 4x4 accessory brands available in southern Africa.
- No fake testimonials, no invented partner logos, no made-up credentials.

## Page copy

- Home: hero slides, value props, featured products, services summary,
  testimonials preview, CTA. Copy already shipped.
- About: owner story (Werner Schaap, founded 2015), workshop photos,
  business hours, location. Copy already shipped.
- Shop: 236 products, search/filter/sort, quote-by-WhatsApp enquiry.
- Services: fitment services with starting prices and durations.
- Gallery: 15 finished builds with vehicle, work done, date.
- Testimonials: 8 verified reviews with name, location, vehicle, date.
- Contact: address, 4 phone numbers, WhatsApp, email, embedded map, form.
